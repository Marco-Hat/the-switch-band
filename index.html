OUR STORY

THE SWITCH represents energy, transformation, and the moment everything changes. When the band takes the stage, it's like flipping a switch — instantly turning an ordinary night into an unforgettable experience.

The name reflects our versatility and ability to seamlessly switch between genres, bringing audiences everything from classic rock, pop, and Latin to dance hits and crowd-favorite cover songs. Half the band grew up steeped in the Philippines' legendary cover-band tradition, the other half brings a French passion for melody and stage presence — six musicians from different corners of the world, united by one obsession. We adapt to every audience while delivering every performance with passion, professionalism, and high energy.

More than just a name, THE SWITCH symbolizes the connection between the band and the crowd. The moment the music starts, people switch from watching to singing, dancing, and celebrating together. Every show is about creating that spark that brings people together and leaves them wanting more.
